STAGES OF A LEGAL MATTER:
1. intake — client initial contact and field collection.
2. scoping — defining the legal area and matter.
3. legal_review — attorneys review the matter and gather documents.
4. quote_sent — a quote has been prepared and sent to the client.
5. quote_accepted — the client accepted the quote.
6. filing — the filing / matter work is underway.
7. closed — the matter is complete.

COSTS / TIMING:
- Quotes are prepared by the legal team after scoping; pricing varies by matter complexity.
- Immigration residency timelines vary by jurisdiction and case type; the team provides case-specific estimates.
- Notary services are typically faster; apostille timelines depend on the issuing authority.

CONTACT:
- The legal team follows up with clients after intake; clients can also book a consultation.
