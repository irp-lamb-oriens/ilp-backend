ILP SALES / CONVERSION KNOWLEDGE (context-sales)

QUALIFICATION:
- A lead is "warm" when they have identified a legal area AND jurisdiction.
- A lead is "hot" when they requested a quote or a consultation booking.

OFFERS:
- Free initial consultation for qualified immigration / real-estate inquiries.
- Fixed-fee quotes for standard incorporation / notary packages.

OBJECTIONS:
- Pricing concern → emphasize fixed-fee transparency and the legal team's jurisdiction-specific expertise.
- Timeline concern → emphasize the team's local filing experience and proactive status updates.
- Trust concern → emphasize attorneys admitted in the target jurisdiction.

NEXT STEPS:
- Guide the client to book a consultation when legalArea + jurisdiction are known.
- For quote requests, connect them to the provider RFQ / quoting flow.
